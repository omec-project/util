The li/ directory (Lawful Interception)
Copyright 2026 Forsway Scandinavia AB

It implements the in-network parts of the 3GPP/ETSI Lawful
Interception architecture for SD-Core: the X1 provisioning interface
(ETSI TS 103 221-1), the X2/X3 delivery framing (ETSI TS 103 221-2), the
TS 33.128 xIRI encoder, the intercept-task store and the LI PKI helpers.

The li/ directory also carries, as test data, ETSI schemas under BSD-3-Clause and
3GPP TS 33.128 attachments (an X1 schema and the xIRI ASN.1 module) under the 3GPP
copyright notice recorded in LICENSES/LicenseRef-3GPP.txt.
