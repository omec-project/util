The files it covers in this repository are two attachments to 3GPP TS 33.128
(Release 18), both vendored unmodified as test data, so that the implementation can be
checked against the published definitions without network access:

    li/x1/testdata/schemas/urn_3GPP_ns_li_3GPPX1Extensions.xsd

the X1 extension schema, byte-identical to the copy 3GPP publishes at
https://forge.3gpp.org/rep/sa3/li/-/raw/main/33128/r18/urn_3GPP_ns_li_3GPPX1Extensions.xsd
and pinned by digest in li/x1/schema_validation_test.go, so a change to it fails the
build; and

    li/iri/testdata/asn1/TS33128Payloads.asn

the xIRI ASN.1 module, whose source and digest are recorded in
li/iri/testdata/asn1/README.md.
